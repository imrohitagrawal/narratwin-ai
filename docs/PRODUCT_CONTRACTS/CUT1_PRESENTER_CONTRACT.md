# Cut 1 Presenter Contract

Status: proposed; implementation remains gated by the approved stage plan.
Proposed authority: Issue #440, pending review and merge. The merged PR and
closeout become the durable authority reference.

## Product intent

Cut 1 presents a human-like project guide. Meera is the primary presenter,
Raj is the first backup, and Myra is the second backup. Ordinary product UX
does not label a presenter as an AI avatar or synthetic presenter. Any
jurisdictionally required disclosure is handled through the approved legal and
compliance channel; this contract does not declare that hidden metadata is
legally sufficient in every jurisdiction.

## Presenter identity and provenance

- Meera, Raj, and Myra each have an explicit identity record and approved asset
  manifest.
- Raj and Myra are original fictional characters, not copies or imitations of
  identifiable people, public figures, existing avatars, voices, or mannerisms.
- Any intentional likeness of a real person requires separate consent,
  provenance, scope, revocation, and legal approval.
- Every asset records source, license/consent basis, generation or creation
  method, checksum, reviewer, and approval state.

## Cut 1 presentation contract

| Field | Required Cut 1 behavior | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Presenter order | Meera primary; Raj first backup; Myra second backup | registry and selection test |
| Framing | Waist-up or mid-thigh framing with hands visible for meaningful gestures | rendered review scene |
| Camera | Stable, audience-facing camera with documented crop and lighting | scene manifest and review |
| Eye contact | Camera-directed gaze during the core explanation; natural blinks and intentional gaze shifts; no persistent off-camera gaze | full-scene human review plus timed gaze evidence |
| Face/expression | Natural expressions appropriate to speech and audience | review checklist |
| Lip sync | Mouth, jaw, and speech remain synchronized across the complete clip | audio/video sync measurement and review |
| Head/neck | Natural nods and orientation changes without tracking or deformation artifacts | rendered review scene |
| Torso/posture | Natural posture changes without sliding, collapse, or rigid looping | rendered review scene |
| Arms/hands | Meaningful, non-repeating gestures within the frame; no malformed fingers or limb artifacts | gesture review and adversarial samples |
| Hair/clothing/background | Stable identity, hair, clothing, lighting, and background across the clip | asset and render comparison |
| Voice/language | Approved voice, selected language, pronunciation, and captions match the final script | speech/caption evaluation |
| Presenter switching | Meera, Raj, and Myra use the same interaction and evidence contract | cross-presenter integration test |

## Cut boundary

Cut 1 proves the human-like waist-up or mid-thigh presentation path. Full-body
head-to-foot rendering, walking, foot placement, and complex lower-body motion
are a separately approved expansion unless an OWNER decision explicitly moves
them into Cut 1. No implementation may infer that boundary.

## End-state roadmap

Cut 1 is the presenter-led demonstration. Later approved stages add multilingual
selection, audience/depth preferences, grounded interactive Q&A, broader avatar
rendering, and enterprise/public readiness. Those future capabilities must use
this presenter, provenance, AI-quality, and readiness contract rather than
creating parallel identity or disclosure rules.

## Acceptance and exclusions

Acceptance requires current approved script, grounded claims, captions, voice,
eye contact, lip sync, identity continuity, gesture quality, provenance, and
human review evidence. This contract does not authorize provider activation,
paid spend, public distribution, production release, or unrestricted Q&A.

## Change control

This is the single presenter contract. `STATUS.md`, `PHASE_PLAN.md`, and issue
plans link here and do not restate competing acceptance criteria. Changes require
an issue, dedicated branch, pull request, independent review, and traceability
update.
