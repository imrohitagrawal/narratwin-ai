# Personal-avatar recording brief

Status: local preparation only; do not upload or enroll yet.

## Enrollment source

Prepare about two continuous minutes of natural English speech, in one real room,
1080p MP4; landscape is preferred for the intended16:9 composition, while
portrait is an eligible enrollment source. Keep the original camera file unretouched. One person only;
stable camera/exposure; even light; quiet room; no music; visible face throughout;
upper body and useful hand framing. Look toward camera and use restrained natural
conversational gestures. Keep wardrobe and room consistent with the intended
first generated passage. Do not introduce a new office plate or generated clothing.

Current provider guidance separates enrollment footage from Avatar V motion
reference. A15-second motion reference does not by itself establish suitable
training footage. Provider-compatible derivative exports, if necessary, must
retain the original and record byte identity and transformation. Verify current
upload path/size limits before export; do not sacrifice the reference silently.

## Human control passage

Separately record an uninterrupted15–30 second passage and save the exact words:

> NarraTwin is being developed to turn approved project knowledge into a grounded
> walkthrough. In this pilot, I am first checking whether a reusable digital
> presenter can preserve my speech, expression and surroundings. The complete
> application demonstration comes after this media test has passed review.

This is preparation language, not a claim that the integrated product already
works. Adjust the text before recording if needed; freeze the exact transcript
with the original recording. Leave clean natural start/end pauses without
trimming words. Initial generated counterpart must use the approved recording's
audio, not new TTS. Preserve waveform/audio derivative lineage and duration.

## Review

Watch/listen to both full original and full generated passage. Check words,
speaker, sync, face/hands, identity, room, wardrobe and clean start/end. Recheck
critical facial passages slowly. Mark each PASS/FAIL/NOT VERIFIED; final acceptance
belongs to Rohit. Contact sheets, metadata and transcripts alone cannot pass.
