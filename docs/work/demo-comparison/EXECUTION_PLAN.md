## Required input architecture before choosing a platform

Realism is not a single prompt field. Every candidate must declare which of these
inputs supplies each visible behavior; `UNRESOLVED` is not replaced by marketing:

1. **Identity** — exact approved face/body/wardrobe references or a separately
   consented training set. Record front/profile/three-quarter/full-body coverage,
   expressions, lighting, resolution, provenance and whether each derivative is
   an approved identity anchor.
2. **Performance** — exact narration, facial action, gaze, head pose, gestures,
   body movement, interaction timing and emotion. State whether these come from
   audio, script, a filmed driver, training footage or generated motion.
3. **World** — a moving real scene or coherently generated scene references,
   including location, other actors, props, product screens, camera, lighting,
   depth, contact, occlusion, shadows and reflections. A static office image is
   a visual reference only unless the exact model jointly animates the scene.
4. **Direction and sequence** — timed shot list, presenter/dialogue ownership,
   camera and lens, action, reaction, continuity, transitions and cut points.
   The target editorial walkthrough keeps narration continuous. Classify every
   frame-time interval once, over the complete delivered-video duration: Meera-
   primary intervals show Meera as an intentional presenter/actor in the main
   composition; cutaway-primary intervals show product/environment without Meera
   as the primary subject. Tiny UI thumbnails/cards do not count as presenter
   footage. Target approximately 55–60% Meera-primary and 40–45% cutaway-primary;
   those shares do not waive complete audio.
5. **Delivery** — aspect, native/delivered pixels, duration, frame/audio codecs,
   captions, soundtrack, deterministic assembly and actual application playback/
   export. Record every transcode, repair and regeneration separately.
6. **Operation and rights** — exact product surface/model revision, API/manual
   distinction, consent, likeness/voice/location/property rights, retention,
   training use, deletion, account eligibility, fan-out and total exposure.

Multiple angles and emotions are platform-specific, not automatically beneficial.
